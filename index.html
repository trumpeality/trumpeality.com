Trumpeality / trump·ee·al·i·tee /
noun  ·  coined 2026
A distorted version of reality, constructed through selective facts, repetition, and absolute conviction, where perception and self-interest consistently override objective truth. Applicable in politics, business, and beyond.
Origin Portmanteau of Trump and reality. Born from the growing realisation that truth itself has become subjective — that what is real and what is fake has become genuinely hard to tell apart. World leaders and billionaires have weaponised this confusion as a tool to serve their self-interests, often at the expense of truth itself. Trumpeality names a phenomenon people already recognise but had no word for. If Truth lives in its own dimension, Trumpeality is the alternative dimension that runs parallel to it.

In business
"The CFO called it a record quarter. The auditors called it something else. A textbook case of corporate Trumpeality."
